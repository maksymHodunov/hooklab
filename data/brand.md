# SockLock brand guide

> Test data for a homework assignment: SockLock and these rules are fictional.

## Product

- Socks with a small magnet in the cuff: a pair stays together in the washer and dryer.
- Subscription: 5 pairs for $29, then a new shipment every quarter.
- Size and style are picked by a quiz (~30 seconds), which also leads to a personal offer.
- Machine washable and dryer safe.

## Audience (US)

- Adults 25–45, often parents, tired of “sock chaos”.
- Main pain: socks get lost in the wash, pairs don't match, the morning starts with hunting for a pair.

## Brand voice

- Friendly, self-ironic, everyday humor. We talk like a friend, not like an ad.
- Short and conversational.
- No fear tactics and no shaming the viewer.

## Don't

- Invent numbers, reviews, ratings, discounts, deadlines or “limited offers”.
- Promise absolute guarantees (“you'll never lose a sock again, guaranteed”).
- Assert the viewer's personal characteristics (“Are you messy?”): Meta's personal attributes policy.
- Make medical or wellness claims: the magnets don't heal anything.
- Mention competitors.
- Use the words revolutionary, life-changing, game-changer.

## Do

- Everyday scenes: laundry, the sock drawer, mornings, kids and partners.
- The price “5 pairs for $29” and the call to take the quiz.
- The magnetic “click” sound as a signature element.
